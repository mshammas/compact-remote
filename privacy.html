Privacy Policy

Effective 29 September 2026

Compact Remote does not collect, store or share any personal data. Everything you set up in the app stays on your device.

Who we are

Compact Remote is an independent app made by Shammas Oliyath (“we”). It lets you control a JBL EON ONE Compact speaker over Bluetooth. It is not made, endorsed or supported by JBL or Harman International.

What the app does with data

  • No personal data is collected. The app has no accounts, sign-in, analytics, advertising, tracking or crash reporting of its own.
  • No data leaves your device through the app. The app does not connect to the internet. It communicates only with your speaker, directly over Bluetooth.
  • Settings stay on your device. Channel names and saved scenes (your mixer settings) are stored locally on your phone, tablet or computer. They are removed if you delete the app.
  • Exports go only where you send them. If you use “Export scenes”, the app creates a file with your scenes and channel names and hands it to your device’s share sheet or save dialog. Where it goes from there (Files, AirDrop, email, a messaging app) is your choice; we never receive it.

Permissions

  • Bluetooth (all platforms; “Nearby devices” on Android): used only to find your speaker and to read and change its settings.
  • Location (Android 11 and earlier only): these Android versions require location permission for any app that scans for Bluetooth devices. Compact Remote does not access, use or store your location.

App stores and your device

Apple (App Store) and Google (Google Play) may collect information such as downloads and crash reports under their own privacy policies. We do not receive personal data from them beyond the anonymous statistics the stores show to all developers.

Children

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts handling personal data, this policy will be updated before that change is released, and the effective date above will change.
