Compact Remote

Mixer, EQ and scenes for the JBL EON ONE Compact, over Bluetooth. For Android, iPhone and Mac.

What you can control

  • Master and channel 1–4 volume, with live updates when you turn the knobs on the speaker
  • Bass and treble for each channel; reverb, chorus and delay sends for channels 1–3
  • Master 8-band EQ with a draggable curve, the speaker's built-in presets (Flat, Speech, Perform, Music) and live-sound starting points
  • 4-band EQ for each channel, with presets by source (vocal mic, speech, acoustic guitar, keyboard, backing track)
  • Ducking (lowers music while the mic is in use)
  • Scenes: save your whole mix and recall it with one tap; export them to a file as a backup or to move them to another device

Getting started

  1. Turn on the speaker and keep it within a few metres.
  2. Close other apps that may be connected to the speaker's controls.
  3. Open Compact Remote, tap Connect to speaker and allow Bluetooth when asked.

No pairing is needed in your phone's Bluetooth settings: the app connects to the speaker's controls directly. Want to look around first? Tap Try demo mode.

Compatibility

Tested with the EON ONE Compact on firmware 6.2.1. Other firmware versions may behave differently; if something doesn't work, please get in touch and include your firmware version (shown at the top of the app once connected).

Questions

The knob lights on the speaker don't follow the app

The speaker's lit knob rings show whichever channel is picked with the CH SELECT button on the speaker. The app's changes still apply to the right channel; the speaker doesn't let apps change which channel its panel shows.

The app can't find my speaker

Check that the speaker is on and nearby, that Bluetooth is on, and that the app has Bluetooth permission (on Android: “Nearby devices”). Only one app can control the speaker at a time, so close other controller apps or disconnect other devices running Compact Remote.

Why are the ducking values plain numbers?

The speaker doesn't report what its ducking values mean in decibels or seconds, so the app shows the speaker's own numbers. Adjust them by ear.

Where are my scenes stored?

On your device only. They aren't synced between devices, and they're removed if you delete the app. To keep a backup or move them to another phone, tablet or Mac, use Export scenes on the Scenes screen, then Import on the other device.
